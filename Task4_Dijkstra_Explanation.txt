TASK 4 – Dijkstra Shortest Path Algorithm

Find the shortest path from Edinburgh to Dundee.

Road Distances:

Edinburgh → Perth = 100 km
Edinburgh → Glasgow = 70 km
Glasgow → Perth = 80 km
Perth → Dundee = 40 km

Possible Routes:

1. Edinburgh → Perth → Dundee
Total distance:
100 + 40 = 140 km

2. Edinburgh → Glasgow → Perth → Dundee
Total distance:
70 + 80 + 40 = 190 km

Shortest Path:

Edinburgh → Perth → Dundee

Total Distance = 140 km
